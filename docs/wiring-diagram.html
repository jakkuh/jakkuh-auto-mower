Mower wiring diagram

Partly bench-tested: relays R1/R2, WiFi link, GPS2, radio and failsafe. Existing wires in real colors, new wires in suggested colors. All grounds tie together.

100%
Wiring notes
  • Hard stop is the key. Key off cuts the main relay and the Pixhawk, and grounds the kill wire, with no software. It only works if you can reach the mower.
  • Never put 24 V on the Pixhawk or its rails. It is 6 V max.
  • Route the SBUS-A and ESP32 antennas out of the steel bay.
  • Unplug the ESP32's USB while it is powered from TELEM1.
  • Keep the kill wire tap short and away from signal wires. Do not probe it with the engine running.
  • Bench-test the relay board before connecting R1 and R2 to the mower.
  • R3 starter: all relays energize for a few seconds at Pixhawk boot. Meter R3 through several power-ups before wiring it to START, or the starter could crank at every key-on.
  • Find the key switch START contact wires with a meter, key off, turning to START.
